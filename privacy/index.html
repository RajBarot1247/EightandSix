Privacy policy

Who I am

Eight & Six is the photography business of Kinjal Naik, based in Providence, Rhode Island, USA. If you have a question about this policy or about your information, email me at hello@[your domain].

What I collect

When you send the contact form, I receive what you type into it: your name and email address and, if you add them, your phone number, the type of shoot, the dates and times, location or venue, guest count and your message. I use this only to reply to you and to plan your shoot. I don't sell it, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

How your message reaches me

The form is sent through Cloudflare, the company that hosts this website, and arrives in my email inbox (Gmail). Before it is sent, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a spam robot, is sending it.

How long I keep it

I keep inquiries in my email for as long as I need them to reply, plan and look after your booking. Ask me at any time and I will delete them.

Visitor statistics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't follow you to other websites.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies for advertising or tracking. Cloudflare may set cookies that are strictly necessary for security, for example to remember that you passed the spam check.

Other services

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Links to Instagram take you to Instagram, where Instagram's own privacy policy applies.

Photos of people

The stories on this site are shared with the permission of the people in them. Photos of children are only published with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, email me and I will take it down.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me at hello@[your domain].

Do Not Track

This website doesn't track you across other websites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date below.
